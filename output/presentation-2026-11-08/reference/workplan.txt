Background (facts, current situation and organisational goals)  
Avans University of Applied Sciences currently has an unfinished open-return wind tunnel available in the 
Aerospace Engineering laboratory. The main mechanical structure of the wind tunnel is already present, but  
the system is not yet fully operational and does not currently provide the measurement and data-acquisition  
functionality required for aerodynamic experiments. 
 
The purpose of this project is to further develop the existing wind tunnel into a functional aerodynamic test  
facility that can be used for educational purposes. The system should allow students to perform practical ex 
periments on airfoils and compare physical measurements with theoretical aerodynamic models. 
A major part of the project is the development and integration of an instrumentation and data-acquisition 
system. Static pressure measurements on an airfoil will be used to determine the pressure distribution over  
the airfoil. These measurements will subsequently be processed into a pressure coefficient 𝐶௣and plotted  
against the dimensionless chord position 𝑥/𝑐. 
 
The experimental results should be compared with theoretical results obtained using XFOIL. In this way, the  
wind tunnel will provide students with a physical test setup that connects aerodynamic theory, simulation,  
measurement technology and data processing.

Scope exclusions (what is outside the project)  
 
-airspeeds above mach 0.3  
-high accuracy measurements  
- Achieving an exact correlation between the theoretical XFOIL simulations and the practical wind-tunnel 
measurements is outside the scope of this project. The project focuses on comparing both results and identi-
fying and explaining possible deviations. 
 
 
Dependencies  
The project depends on the available project time, budget and delivery times of required components. Long  
or unexpected delivery times for sensors, electronics or mechanical components may delay the  
implementation and testing phases. 
The available budget limits the selection of components. Where possible, existing hardware and laboratory 
equipment will therefore be reused to reduce both costs and lead times.
